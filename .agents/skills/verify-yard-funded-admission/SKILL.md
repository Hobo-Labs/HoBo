# Funded Yard admission and generation

**Properties:** A funded Yard receipt requires a quote for its selected Yard and provider, an unchanged quote and budget context around policy and reservation, a policy proof bound to the complete quote digest and ID, a quote bound to selected resource work and any generation plan, and a current quote and context through receipt issuance. If evidence changes after the durable reservation, the hold remains for reconciliation. Generation reserves durable quota after host approval and must claim funding under current context; on the covered failures after a provider effect, the original hold is marked uncertain, or a failed uncertainty write is reported while the hold remains. A failure can issue a receipt for different or expired liability, provision without valid funding, or leave a provider effect without identified reconciliation.

**Review baseline:** commit `709ea76715f765cd0d88690bda8dd8fee4f148b8`. PASS for the bounded models, symbolic abstraction and generated tests below. The request parser refuses malformed constraints and estimates before this admission flow; `verify-yard-request-validation` checks that boundary separately. `verify-yard-json-constraints` checks structural property matching, and `verify-yard-residency-validation` checks residency preflight before admission. The separate `verify-yard-camp-constraints` skill checks refusal of unverified Camp and nested-layout constraints. The separate `verify-yard-generation-policy-gate` skill checks unsupported generation effects, unavailable approval or attestation, and property locks.

## Covered code

- `packages/broker/src/yard-placement/deploy.ts` snapshots Yard quotes and budget, broker, catalog, tenant, policy and authority context; matches the selected Yard and provider; rechecks evidence and freshness across callbacks and reservation; retains committed holds on refusal; and marks the original generation hold uncertain on the covered post-effect failures. Generation checks the plan content against its approved hash before claim and provider operation. The trusted durable authority owns reservation atomicity and balance accounting.
- `packages/broker/src/budget-authority.ts` computes the protobuf JSON SHA-256 quote digest shared by policy admission and durable reservations; the protobuf and CUE policy-proof schema carry the digest and reject unbound proofs at admission.
- `packages/broker/src/rejections.ts` records the admission and reconciliation rejection codes. `packages/broker/test/yard-funded-quote-race.test.ts` exercises a real SQLite authority, foreign Yard and provider quotes, mutations around policy and reservation, expiry, final receipt races and 100 generated Boolean combinations (seed 614525). `yard-event-store-folds.test.ts` covers unavailable budget evidence, changed context before claim and after provisioning, failed uncertainty writes, missing residual definitions, unavailable provider signals and refusal of a foreign Camp or invalid residency before any hold or provider effect. `hosting-budget.test.ts` exercises funded admission and settlement integration. `yard-policy-quote-binding.test.ts` checks legacy and foreign proofs, same-ID quote changes, binary wire round trips, and 100 generated cases (seed 614526). `yard-quote-work-binding.test.ts` checks stale definitions, prices, catalog, request, broker and Yard, with 80 generated cases (seed 614527); generation tests cover changed plans before claim and provider effect, changed work after effect, and separately funded local generation.

## Scope and evidence

`YardFundedAdmission` explores 274 states over abstract selection, quote and context checks before and after reservation, freshness, the final receipt handoff and retained holds. Its six-state projection hides 128 same-label steps. `YardFundedGeneration` explores 40 states for funding, context at claim, a provider effect, subsequent evidence and uncertainty-write availability. Its six-state projection hides 16 same-label steps. `YardPolicyQuote` explores 24 states for two quote contents, matching or foreign IDs, and a legacy unbound proof. Its three-state projection hides 12 same-label steps. `YardQuotedWork` explores 92 states in the generation path for quote/work agreement, plan checks before claim and effect, later work changes and uncertainty writes. Its eight-state projection hides 32 same-label steps. Its reserve step abstracts the separate work check, policy callback and atomic hold; the other models and real-code race tests cover selected intervening changes. Its provider step abstracts an external operation. Eight admission, five generation, two quote-proof and six work model-guard mutations produce counterexamples. Z3 checks eight admission guard implications and a quote-binding implication over arbitrary abstract quote values; the latter assumes honest proof binding and collision resistance for the compared quotes. A separate Z3 obligation proves work equality for arbitrary abstract work values under truthful bindings and pairwise collision resistance. Twenty-one weakened symbolic premises produce counterexamples. CUE checks accept a valid policy digest and refuse missing or malformed policy digests; generic non-Yard quotes may omit work digest, while malformed present work digests are refused. These abstractions do not establish that TypeScript implements them.

The real-code regression suite uses SQLite and exercises independent quote, context, reservation and post-effect mutations. Twenty-seven real-code mutations first run a passing unmodified control, then remove guards in an isolated package and require the corresponding running tests to fail; collection errors are refused. Single removals of the earlier generation funding and pre-effect checks are masked by the quota and provider-boundary checks and are not counted. Run them with `python3 .agents/skills/verify-yard-funded-admission/mutations/real-code.py`. The quote binds the selected work and generation plan; the callback, provider prices and catalog remain trusted assertions. `verify-yard-generation-quota` checks the separate quota authority and admission. `verify-yard-generation-authority` separately checks changed generation policy, runtime catalog and connector evidence; `verify-yard-generation-state` checks identity, readiness and provider signal batches. This skill assumes truthful policy, atomic durable authority and provider evidence; it does not authenticate the host, prove actual provider bills or remote execution, cover every exception after a provider effect, prove TypeScript refinement, or establish liveness. Transport and receiver-side enforcement require separate verification.

For an implementation mutation, copy `packages/broker` to a scratch package with linked `node_modules`, set `BROKER_YARD_FUNDED_VERIFY_PACKAGE_ROOT`, and remove the first policy quote equality check. The generation path also has a later check after quota reservation. The real-code test must fail. Restore the source and rerun the unmodified skill.
