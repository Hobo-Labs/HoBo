# `@hobo/backend-routing`

Cost-based choosing for a one-active fleet: whether to keep the active member,
hold, switch to another or fail over, and which eligible member to prefer, by
what each member costs. Nothing here knows an engine.

Which member *is* active is not decided here. The assignment register (the
majority-decided log of who is active), the cache-key replica, member health
and the one-active loop moved to `@hobo/fleet`
([design §6.10](../../docs/design/index.md)): `@hobo/fleet/assignment` holds
the register under the names it always had (`proposeAssignment`,
`readCurrentAssignment`, `ephemeralAssignmentReplica`, …, with
`AssignmentEntry` for `FleetAssignment`), and `@hobo/fleet/observation` holds
`eligibleForActive`, the one place a member's health is judged. A host runs
`createOneActiveFleet` and passes `decideFleet` or `routeFleetByBudget` as its
`chooseAction`; `@hobo/data-postgres` does the first, the hosted CI relay's
cache fleet the second.

## What this package owns

| Concern | Entry point |
|---|---|
| Billing periods and usage units | `billingPeriod`, `FLEET_USAGE_UNITS` |
| Keep, switch or fail over a database fleet | `decideFleet`, `parseFleetFinancialModel`, `parseFleetPolicy` |
| Route a loss-tolerant fleet by budget | `routeFleetByBudget`, `parseFleetOffer`, `parseFleetRoutingMarks` |

Both functions take the fleet's observations as `NamedObservation`s
(`{ name, observation }`, `@hobo/fleet`) and the current `AssignmentEntry`;
their names, outputs and ranking are what they were before the move. Each asks
`eligibleForActive` whether a member is healthy enough (mode `failover` when
the active member is failing, `switch` otherwise) and keeps the reasons it
gives: `unreachable`, `quota-exhausted`, `auth`, `unknown`, `never-copied`,
`lag 21m exceeds rpo 20m`, and `not-ready`, which `decideFleet` reports as its
`schema-not-current`. The health thresholds `rpoMs`, `failoverAfterMs` and
`FleetRoutingMarks.failoverAfterMs` stay input fields; a host fills them from
the fleet's `FleetPolicy` (`recovery_point_ms`, `failover_after_ms`). The money
thresholds stay here.

## Budget routing

`routeFleetByBudget` is for a fleet whose state may be lost, so a move needs no
copy and has no lag to judge: free, then prepaid, then the cheapest
pay-as-you-go member; the active member is left when it is projected to reach
`switchAt` (default 90%) of an allowance it would stop at, or would exceed its
budget cap; any other member is a candidate only below `switchBackBelow`
(default 80%), so a member left for its allowance is not chosen again within
the month. A move for preference alone needs a cheaper tier or month and
waits out `minDwellMs`. No cap means no money. A member failing longer than
`failoverAfterMs` is left for the best reachable one that is not past a stop
or a cap. Unknown is never a reason to move.

`decideFleet` is the database fleet's policy: a planned switch only to a
standby that is provably affordable with headroom, a failover to the freshest
standby within the recovery point, preferring an affordable one over a fresher
one that is not.

## Gates

- **TypeScript:** `pnpm --filter @hobo/backend-routing run build`.
- **Protobuf + CUE:** `data-models/backend_routing.proto` (package
  `hobo.backend_routing.v1`: usage, financial models, offers, marks, decisions
  and routes) with `backend_routing.cue` constraining every message;
  `src/_generated/` is generated by `pnpm run codegen`. Parse, don't
  validate: the TypeScript records are parsed derived types, and
  `parseFleetFinancialModel`, `parseFleetOffer` and `parseFleetRoutingMarks`
  are the only ways in.
- **PACT:** [`contracts/one-active-choose-action.pact.json`](./contracts/README.md),
  the `chooseAction` boundary of the one-active loop, replayed by
  `test/choose-action-pact.test.ts`. The cache replica's pact moved with the
  register to `packages/fleet/contracts/`.
- **Tests:** `test/routing.test.ts`; `decideFleet`'s cases run in
  `packages/data-postgres/test/fleet-policy.test.ts`. Both keep every test
  title and `expect` statement they had before observations became
  `NamedObservation`s (`pnpm run fleet:moved-tests -- O17`).

## Architecture placement

A policy library, not a domain service. It handles no command and emits no
events, runs no actor, and keeps no event store, projection, materialized
view, snapshot or read store: a host asks it, logs the decision, and moves the
fleet through the register in `@hobo/fleet`.
