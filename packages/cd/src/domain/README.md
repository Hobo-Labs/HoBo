# `@hobo/cd/domain`

`@hobo/cd/domain` models continuous delivery Freight lineage, Stage promotion pipelines, pluggable pull-based delivery flows, promotions, and multi-camp wave fanout on `@alchemy.run/domain` with branded Effect schemas (`CdPromotionId`, `CdFanoutId`, `CampTargetId`, `FreightDigest`, `WarehouseId`, `FreightAlias`, `StageId`, `DeliveryRunId`, `RevisionRef`, `PullRequestId`, `PullRequestUrl`, `BranchName`, `DeliveryFlowTarget`, `ApproverId`, `RealizationRef`, `NonEmptyReason`, `NonNegativeCount`, `PositiveCount`, `EpochMs`, `UniqueCampWave`, `UniqueStageList`) and discriminated tagged-union states:

- **Aggregates**:
  - `CdFreight` (`Unrecorded | Active | Yanked`) tracks Warehouse-discovered immutable Freight bundles, human-readable aliases (`SetFreightAlias`), per-stage qualifications (`RecordFreightQualification`), expiring manual approval bypasses (`ApproveFreightForStage`), and yanking (`YankFreight`).
  - `CdStage` (`Unconfigured | Idle | Promoting | Verifying`) models pipeline Stages with upstream lineage (`direct | one-of | all | quorum`), required soak durations (`SoakTimeNotElapsed`), auto-promotion policies (`manual | newest | match-upstream`), pluggable delivery flows (`gitops-pull | gitops-pr-pull | agent-pull | conductor-direct | release-orchestrator`), single-active-promotion queueing with supersession (`StagePromotionSuperseded`), re-verification (`ReverifyStageFreight`), and abort (`AbortStageVerification`).
  - `CdDeliveryRun` (`Idle | PendingApproval | Publishing | AwaitingPullRequest | AwaitingConvergence | Verifying | Completed | RolledBack | Failed`) tracks pull-based and direct delivery runs across commit/PR publication, PR merge, pull-engine convergence, implicit or explicit verification, completion, and fail-closed rollback.
  - `CdPromotion` (`Idle | PendingApproval | Approved | Realized | Verified | Completed | RolledBack | Failed`) and `CdFanout` (`Idle | Running | Completed | Halted`) model single-camp promotions and wave fanout with failure budgets.
- **Policies, Sagas & External Systems**: `StagePromotionToDeliveryRunPolicy` and `StartCdDeliveryFlowPolicy` launch `ExecuteCdDeliveryFlow` against the pluggable `CdDeliveryFlow` and `CdVerificationGate` ports, suspending durably on `PullRequestResolvedSignal` and `ConvergenceObservedSignal` (`DurableDeferred`) when a pull request is open or a pull engine is still converging, failing closed on closed PRs, degraded convergence, or unconfirmed rollbacks, and qualifying Freight + advancing queued Stage promotions on PASS. `StartCdPromotionPolicy` coordinates `ExecuteCdPromotion` against `CdCampRealizer` and `CdVerificationGate`.
- **Views & API**: discriminated `CdFreightCatalogView`, `CdStageStatusView`, `CdDeliveryRunStatusView`, `CdPromotionStatusView`, `CdFanoutStatusView`, and `CdReleaseApi` expose materialized views and projections, snapshots, and read store queries over in-process and HTTP transports.

## Runbook

- **Stage promotion rejected with `SoakTimeNotElapsed` or `FreightNotEligibleForStage`**: inspect `CdFreightCatalogView` qualifications and upstream `CdStageStatusView` `lastVerifiedAtMs` before retrying, or grant an expiring `ApproveFreightForStage` for a governed hotfix.
- **Delivery run parked in `AwaitingPullRequest` or `AwaitingConvergence`**: verify PR merge status or target pull-engine sync/health status, then deliver `PullRequestResolvedSignal` or `ConvergenceObservedSignal` (or let the Rivet pull-watch actor resolve it).
- **Promotion rejected with `RealizationRequiredBeforeVerify` or `RollbackRequiresRealization`**: verify the promotion state in `CdPromotionStatusView` before issuing verification or rollback commands.
- **Fanout rejected with `CampNotInWave` or `CampOutcomeAlreadyRecorded`**: check the wave's `campIds` from `CdFanoutStarted`; each camp target may report an outcome at most once.
