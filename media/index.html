Bo — pixel reference

Bo's model sheet on a 48px grid — turnaround, poses and expressions, flat black and white with a 1px dual rim so he reads on light and dark grounds alike. Not generated: the bodies are a deterministic downscale of the shipped sheets above and the poses are pixel surgery on those bases, rebuilt by media/scripts/build-bo-pixel-reference.py — so the pixel Bo cannot drift from the real one. The north view is derived (front stand mirrored, enclosed whites inked out); east and west remain separate drawings, never mirrors of each other.

The cast — hobits who are not Bo

Four new characters for the site's troupe: the travelers who turn up around Bo when the page has been still a while. Generated with the Higgsfield CLI (GPT Image 2.6, native transparency), reviewed, and normalized by media/scripts/build-trail-art.py to one sheet contract. These are pose sheets, not cycles — the player steps through all twelve cells so each can be inspected. Cells 1–3 are derived from cell 0 by moving colour only, so a character can look around without its outline boiling.

A textured mesh lifted from the four turnaround views, for use as a pose and lighting reference when generating video — not as a shipping asset. Symmetry enforcement was turned off during the lift: it would have "corrected" the bindle by giving him two, or by deleting it.
